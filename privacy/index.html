Ghost Words offers solo and local pass-and-play word games. Public accounts, online multiplayer, and cloud saves are disabled for this release. The game does not send local match progress to a Ghost Words multiplayer server.

Data stored on the device

Settings, tutorial completion, progress, match records, daily results, collected items, revealed-word journal, Visions balance, and advertising privacy choices are stored locally using Unity PlayerPrefs. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes this local data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. There is no developer-operated cloud recovery for these saves.

Optional rewarded advertising

The app includes Unity LevelPlay 9.5.0 for optional rewarded video advertising. When advertising is enabled, choosing an available Earn Visions action can show an ad; a completed ad grants three Visions. Watching ads is optional.

Advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device/app information, and ad delivery and interaction events. Those services use information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements, personalized advertising. Limited ads do not mean that no data is processed. Processing and retention by Unity and any enabled advertising partners are described in their policies.

See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for provider information, rights, and privacy request options. The final release disclosures must reflect the advertising networks actually included and enabled.

Settings > Privacy Choices provides personalized/limited advertising and do-not-sell/share preferences that the app passes to LevelPlay. Android also provides advertising privacy controls in device settings. The production consent flow and applicable regional disclosures must be reviewed before ads are enabled. The checked-in development configuration has production advertising disabled; this is not a claim that an ad-enabled release collects no data.

Other services

Separate Unity Analytics, Crash Reporting, Purchasing, and Performance Reporting integrations are disabled in this project's Unity Services settings. This does not disable the separately integrated LevelPlay advertising SDK. The submitted app's permissions and SDK behavior must match its published Data Safety answers.

Children

The advertising integration is configured for a general audience and is not configured as child-directed. The publisher must confirm the intended audience, age/content rating, and applicable regional requirements before publication.
