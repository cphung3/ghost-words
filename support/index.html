Help & support

A few useful answers, and what to include when something goes wrong.

Support contact is being prepared.

A dedicated support and privacy contact will be added here before the app is publicly released.

Report a problem

When contacting support, include:

  • Your device model, Android version, and the app version.
  • The mode you were playing and the steps that led to the problem.
  • What you expected and what happened. A screenshot can help, if it does not contain information you want to keep private.

Where is my progress saved?

Settings, progress, records, and your Visions balance are saved on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove them. There is no developer-operated cloud recovery for these saves.

Can I play with friends online?

The planned first release supports solo play and local pass-and-play for two to four people. Online multiplayer is disabled.

What happens when an ad is unavailable?

Rewarded ads are optional. Availability can vary with connectivity, privacy choices, and ad inventory. Try again later; completing an available rewarded ad grants three Visions.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy for local storage, advertising, and privacy choices. For information processed by Unity’s advertising services, the policy also links to the provider’s privacy information.
